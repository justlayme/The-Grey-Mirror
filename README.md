# The Grey Mirror

Grey Mirror is a relationship text analyzer from Wentropy Labs. It reads a complete conversation history and shows the communication patterns across the whole timeline, with dated evidence and confidence context.

This repository is a public overview. The product runs at [thegreymirror.com](https://thegreymirror.com/grey-mirror).

## What it does

- Measures reply timing, initiation, reciprocity, repair and conflict.
- Shows how those patterns change across the available history.
- Links findings to evidence where it can, and leaves out what it cannot support.
- Offers a full report with a timeline, cinematic narrative, Memory Lane, metrics and custom charts.

## What it reads

- iMessage or SMS exports
- WhatsApp exports
- Instagram message exports
- CSV, TXT, JSON and ZIP files

On iPhone, you can also import one Messages conversation directly through an Apple Shortcut, with no Mac, screenshots or export file.

## How to start

1. Open [Grey Mirror](https://thegreymirror.com/grey-mirror).
2. Add a conversation, or import one from your iPhone.
3. Read your free preview. It includes 4 evidence-linked findings, and no card is required.

Analysis runs on Grey Mirror's servers after upload. It is not local-only processing. The [privacy checklist](https://thegreymirror.com/grey-mirror/relationship-text-analyzer-privacy-checklist) covers what to look for in any tool like this.

## What it is not

Grey Mirror is interpretive pattern analysis. It is not therapy, diagnosis, legal advice, medical advice, crisis support or a lie detector.
